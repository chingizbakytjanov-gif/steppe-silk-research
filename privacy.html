Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

This site does not use cookies or advertising trackers, and it has no accounts or forms. It counts visits anonymously.

What is collected

  • Server logs. The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel records technical data such as your IP address, browser and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Visit statistics. Page views are counted with Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and does not follow you across sites or visits. It records the page opened, the site you came from, your country, and the type of browser and device, and shows them only as totals.
  • Fonts. The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page opens.
  • Email. If you write to chingiz.bakytjanov@gmail.com, your message and address are kept only to reply to you and are never shared or sold.

Your rights

You can ask what information about you is held and ask for it to be deleted by writing to the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
