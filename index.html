Fewer Cars, Cheaper Oil: China's Trade with Russia, 2020-2025

Trade fell 6.9% in 2025, to $228.1 billion. China's car exports to Russia almost halved.

According to the General Administration of Customs of China, trade between China and Russia fell 6.9% in 2025, to $228.1 billion. It was the first fall since 2020. China's exports to Russia fell 10.4%, to $103.3 billion, and its imports from Russia fell 3.9%, to $124.8 billion. Vehicles explain almost all of the fall in exports: they dropped from $25.5 billion to $13.1 billion. On the import side, oil, gas and coal fell by $16.3 billion, while metals and ores rose. Russia has not published its own trade by country since 2022.
