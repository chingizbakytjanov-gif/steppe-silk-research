I am not an expert on Russia, so I will only say what these numbers mean to me. The fall in car exports shows how fast trade with China can change: in three years Russia went from buying $4 billion of Chinese vehicles to $25 billion, and then half of that disappeared in one year. Russia now sells China mostly oil, gas and coal. This is the same weakness I wrote about in Kazakhstan's trade with China, only on a larger scale. For Kazakhstan I see a lesson and a chance. The lesson is not to depend on one big buyer for our raw materials. The chance is that Kazakhstan sits between China and Russia, so a good transport network can earn money from their trade, whatever happens to it.
