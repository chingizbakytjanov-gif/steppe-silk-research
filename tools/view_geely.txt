Of all the companies I have looked at, Geely's last year seems the most encouraging to me. When car sales in China fell, the company did not wait for the market to recover. It sold more abroad and moved to more expensive cars, and its core profit grew. Buying out Zeekr also makes sense: one company with shared factories and engineers is cheaper to run than several brands that compete with each other. For Kazakhstan, Geely is the kind of Chinese investment I would like to see more of, because it builds cars here instead of only shipping them in. My two concerns are the target of 3.45 million cars for 2026, which looks too high after nine months, and Lynk &amp; Co, whose sales keep falling.
