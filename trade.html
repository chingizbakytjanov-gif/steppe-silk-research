Raw Materials Out, Machines In: Kazakhstan's Trade with China, 2020–2025

Trade between Kazakhstan and China more than doubled, from $15.4 billion to $34.1 billion. Kazakhstan still sells mostly raw materials and buys machinery, electronics and vehicles. Since 2023 it has run a trade deficit with China, which reached $3.7 billion in 2025.
