About

Steppe & Silk Research publishes independent research on companies and trade in Kazakhstan, Central Asia and China.

There are two formats. Company notes explain how a company makes money and what has changed, based on its annual report. Trade briefs show what Kazakhstan sells and buys, based on official statistics.

How the research is made

  • Numbers come from annual reports and official statistics, and every source is linked.
  • The data behind each chart can be downloaded.
  • When two sources disagree, as Kazakh and Chinese trade data do, the report says whose numbers it uses.
  • Opinions are kept apart from facts, in a section called "My view".
  • Nothing on this site is investment advice.

Corrections

If you find an error, please email me. Corrections are made openly and listed at the end of the report with their date.
