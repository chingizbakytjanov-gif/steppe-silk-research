About

A student's research site about finance in Kazakhstan and China

Steppe & Silk Research explains finance through real companies and real trade figures, for anyone who is interested, whether they are new to the subject or already work in it. Its focus is Kazakhstan and the economic ties between Kazakhstan, Central Asia and China.

Who makes it

The site is written and built by Chingiz Bakytzhanov, a first-year university student in Astana who wants to work in finance. He started it in October 2026. Each report comes from reading company reports and trade statistics himself, checking the numbers and writing up what they show. He works in Kazakh, Russian and English, which helps with sources that are not published in English.

It is a student project in the way that matters most: it is where finance is learned in public. Each report is a piece of homework that anyone can check, because the sources are linked and the data can be downloaded.

Why Kazakhstan and China

China is Kazakhstan's largest trading partner, Kazakh companies sell uranium, oil and metals to Chinese buyers, and airlines, banks and carmakers on both sides are building new links. Much of this is reported in Russian, Kazakh or Chinese, or not explained at all. The site tries to make it readable for everyone.

How a report is made

  1. Read the sourceEvery report starts with a primary document: an annual report, a results release, a stock exchange filing or a customs table.
  2. Check every numberFigures are copied from the source, then checked against a second source where one exists. Where Kazakh and Chinese statistics disagree, both are shown.
  3. Draw it and share itEach chart is built from a spreadsheet that readers can download, so anyone can test the result.
  4. Write it plainlyReports explain what the numbers mean in plain English, cite every source and keep opinion in a separate section.

House rules

  • Every figure links to its source.
  • The data behind each chart can be downloaded.
  • When sources disagree, the report says which figure it uses and why.
  • The author's own opinion appears only under "My view" or "Author's comment".
  • Nothing here is investment advice.
