Purpose

China is the largest trading partner of each Central Asian country, and Kazakh companies sell uranium, oil and metals to Chinese buyers. Much of the information about these links is published in Russian, Kazakh or Chinese, or spread across filings that few readers open. The publication brings it together in English, with the figures checked and the sources linked.

Editor

The publication is edited and written by Chingiz Bakytzhanov, a first-year university student in Astana. It is an independent student project and is not affiliated with any company, university or government body.

Method

  1. Each report starts from a primary source: an annual report, a results release, a stock exchange filing or a customs table.
  2. Figures are taken from that source and checked against a second source where one exists.
  3. Where national statistics disagree, as Kazakh and Chinese trade data often do, both series are shown and the choice is explained.
  4. Every chart is built from a spreadsheet that is published with the report.
  5. Opinion is kept to a separate, labelled section at the end of a report.

Standards

  • Every figure links to its source.
  • The data behind each chart can be downloaded.
  • Nothing published here is investment advice.
